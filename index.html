Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Run Story app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Finish Line Labs, LLC (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not directly collect personally identifiable information. To function, it relies on the following:

  • Strava account connection. You authenticate with your Strava account using Strava's OAuth. The Application receives an access token and uses it to fetch your activity data (distance, time, route, photos, etc.) from Strava on your behalf. Activity data is cached locally on your device so the Application can display it offline.
  • Strava webhook. The Application uses a small webhook service that listens for activity-update notifications from Strava (for example, when you upload a new activity or add a photo). The webhook stores the notification payload (a minimal record identifying which activity changed) only long enough for the Application to fetch it. Once the Application retrieves the notification, the record is deleted from the server. No activity content is stored server-side — only the change notification itself, and only temporarily.
  • Advertising. The Application displays banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data in accordance with Google's privacy policy.
  • Analytics. The Application uses Google Firebase Analytics to record anonymous, aggregate usage events (for example, which features are used) to help improve the Application.
  • Maps. The Application renders maps using the Google Maps SDK. Standard device and request information may be transmitted to Google in accordance with Google's privacy policy.
  • Notifications. If you opt in to the weekly summary notification, the Application schedules a local notification on your device. No notification content is sent through a remote server.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not access your device's location services or collect real-time location data. The only location information the Application sees is whatever is already attached to your Strava activities (which you control through Strava).


Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

The Application relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Strava (account authentication and activity data)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage analytics)
  • Google Maps Platform (map rendering)

No user data is sold, rented, or shared with any other third party.


What are my opt-out rights?

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it through the standard uninstall process on your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace. You can also revoke the Application's access to your Strava account at any time from Strava's "My Apps" settings.

The webhook service retains data only for the brief window required to deliver an update notification to your device, after which the record is deleted.
