Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

Finish Line Labs, LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the On the Run! mobile application (the "App"), available for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

In short: We do not run any servers and we do not receive your data. The App reads your step and distance totals on your device to award in-game rewards. Your game progress can be backed up to your own Google or Apple cloud account, which we cannot read. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking in the App.

Information the App Uses

Health and Fitness Data

If you choose to connect it, the App reads the following from your device's health service — Health Connect on Android, or Apple Health on iOS:

  • Step count for the current day
  • Distance walked or run for the current day
  • Number of exercise or workout sessions for the current day

This is used for one purpose: converting your real-world activity into in-game Race Coins and Stamina. The App reads this data only; it never writes to, edits, or deletes anything in your health service.

Health data is read on your device and used on your device. It is not sent to us, and we never see it. The App does not read your heart rate, sleep, weight, GPS routes, location, or any health category beyond the three listed above.

Connecting your health data is optional. The App is fully playable without it; you simply earn rewards from play alone.

Game Progress

The App stores your progress on your device. This includes levels completed, medals earned, Race Coins, Stamina, Fuel and cosmetic items, which coach messages you have seen, and a per-day record of which activity rewards you have already claimed.

That last item is worth being specific about: the daily record notes which step milestones you reached on a given date and how many miles were credited to you, so the App does not pay you twice for the same walk. It is derived from your health data, and it is included in the cloud backup described below.

Cloud Backup

So that progress survives a lost or replaced phone, the App can back your save file up to your own cloud storage account:

  • Android: Google Play Games Services (Saved Games), tied to your Google account.
  • iOS: Apple iCloud (CloudKit private database), tied to your Apple Account.

This backup contains the game progress described above, including the daily reward records. It does not contain your raw step counts, distances, workout history, name, email address, or contact details.

These are your accounts, not ours. The data goes directly from your device to Google or Apple under their terms and privacy policies. We have no server, no database, and no ability to access, read, or retrieve your save file. Your use of those services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Cloud backup is optional and can be declined or turned off. The App works without it, with progress kept on your device only.

What the App Does Not Do

To be explicit, the App contains none of the following:

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No crash or performance reporting services
  • No third-party marketing, attribution, or social media SDKs
  • No account creation, login, name, email address, or phone number
  • No access to location, GPS, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts

Your health and fitness data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold, rented, or transferred to any third party. We do not sell or share personal information under any circumstances.

Purchases

The current version of the App contains no in-app purchases and no payment processing. Should paid items be added in a future version, purchases would be handled entirely by the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store, which process payment details directly — we would not receive or store your payment information. This policy would be updated before any such feature ships.

Artificial Intelligence

AI-powered tools (such as Claude by Anthropic) were used to assist with the development, artwork, and coding of the App. The App itself does not use, incorporate, or rely on any artificial intelligence or machine learning system in its functionality. Your data is never processed by or sent to any AI system.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: Uninstalling the App removes your local save data.
  • Health permissions: You can revoke the App's access at any time — on Android in Health Connect settings, on iOS in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health. Revoking access stops the App reading any further data. The App keeps working.
  • Cloud backup: Because the backup lives in your own account, you control it. On Android, delete it through Google Play Games (Settings › Delete Play Games account data). On iOS, remove the App's iCloud data through Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, and no data deletion request to make of us.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App collects no personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised effective date, and posted before the change takes effect in the App.
