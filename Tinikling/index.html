Tinikling

The Philippine bamboo dance, under your fingers. For iPhone and Android.

Your fingers are your feet. Step in and out of the clapping bamboo poles on the beat of Filipino folk songs, and don't get caught when they snap shut.

In the game

  • The Journey: from your lola's yard to the town fiesta, dancing against rival dancers stop by stop.
  • Five folk songs: Bahay Kubo, Sa Bukid, Atin Cu Pung Singsing, Lulay and Tinikling, each in its own place, on Easy, Normal and Hard.
  • Versus: two players on one phone, or a room of up to eight online with a code.
  • Lola's lesson to learn the step, Practice, and achievements in Game Center and Google Play Games.
  • Free to play, with no ads. Optional in-app purchases.
