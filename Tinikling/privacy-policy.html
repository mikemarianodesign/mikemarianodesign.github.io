Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Finish Line Labs, LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Tinikling mobile application (the "App"), available for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

In short: Tinikling is free, with no advertising. We run no servers of our own. Your progress stays on your device and is backed up to your own iCloud or Google Play Games account, which we cannot read; achievements are also shown in Game Center or Google Play Games if you use them. We collect anonymous gameplay analytics (how the game is played) to balance the game, and you can switch that off in Settings. Online rooms connect you to your friends through Unity's game services using an anonymous ID, and the name you type and your scores are shown only to the other dancers in your room.

Information the App Uses

Game Progress

The App stores your progress on your device: your Journey stars and lives, your best score and stars for each song and level, your achievements, whether you have had Lola's lesson, your timing calibration and your settings, along with the name you last typed for online rooms.

Cloud Backup

So that your progress survives a new or replaced device, and is the same on all your devices, the App keeps a copy of it in your own cloud account:

  • iOS: Apple iCloud, tied to your Apple Account.
  • Android: Google Play Games (Saved Games), tied to your Google account, when you are signed in to Play Games.

The copy contains the game progress described above. It does not contain your settings, contact details, or anything else.

These are your accounts, not ours. The data goes directly from your device to Apple or Google under their terms and privacy policies, and we have no ability to access, read, or retrieve it. You can turn off iCloud for the App in your device's settings, or delete your Play Games data through Google Play Games; the App then keeps your progress on your device only.

Achievements

The App has achievements (for example, clearing a chapter of the Journey). They are kept on your device, and if you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the App also reports the achievements you earn to that service so they appear in your profile there. The App signs in automatically when you open it if you already use the service on your device; you can sign out or turn it off in your device's settings, and the App works the same without it.

The App receives only what it needs to show that you are signed in. It does not receive your email address, contacts, or friends list. Game Center is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google Play Games by Google's Privacy Policy.

Online rooms

If you choose to dance a song online with friends, the App uses Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication, Multiplayer and Relay), operated by Unity Technologies, to connect the two phones:

  • An anonymous player ID. The first time you play online, Unity Authentication creates a random identifier for your copy of the App. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any account.
  • The room. Creating a room makes a short room code that your friends type to join. Unity's Relay service passes game messages between the phones in the room (who is there, the song, and each dancer's score as it changes), and the room closes when its host leaves.
  • Your name and scores. You enter a name to show in the room. It is sent only to the other dancers in your room, for as long as the room lasts, along with your score in each song. It is not stored by us or kept after the room closes.

Unity processes this connection information, along with standard technical information such as your IP address, under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Playing alone (the Journey, Practice, Versus on one phone and Lola's lesson) does not use these services at all.

Analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, so that it can be balanced and problems found. This is limited to gameplay. The App can only send the following events, and there is no mechanism in it for sending anything else:

  • Lola's lesson started, finished or skipped (and at which step)
  • A song finished: which song and level, whether it was Practice, the Journey or an online room, the stars, the accuracy in tenths, and whether you were caught out
  • A Journey stop danced: which stop, whether it was cleared, the stars and the lives left
  • Running out of lives, the refill being offered, and a refill bought
  • A Versus on one phone finished: which song and level
  • An online room made or joined, and a song danced in one (how many dancers)
  • Timing calibrated (the delay found, in milliseconds)
  • An achievement earned (which one)

Nothing you type is ever sent. Firebase itself collects standard technical information such as a randomly generated app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, and an approximate region derived from your IP address, as described in Google's Firebase Privacy documentation. The App does not collect or send an advertising identifier: the advertising permissions Firebase would normally add are removed from the app before it is built, and it does not use Apple's advertising identifier.

You can turn analytics off. Open Settings in the App and switch ANALYTICS to OFF. It is on by default; switching it off stops collection on that device immediately.

What the App Does Not Do

To be explicit, the App contains none of the following:

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier
  • No crash reporting, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No third-party marketing, attribution, or social media SDKs
  • No account creation or login, and no collection of your email address or phone number
  • No chat between players
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts
  • No servers of our own, and no database holding anything about you

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information under any circumstances.

Artificial Intelligence

AI-powered tools (such as Claude by Anthropic) were used to assist with the development, artwork, and coding of the App. The rival dancers you dance against in the Journey are ordinary game logic running on your device. The App does not use or rely on any artificial intelligence or machine learning service, and your data is never sent to one.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: Uninstalling the App removes your progress from the device.
  • Cloud backup: Because the backup lives in your own account, you control it. On iOS, remove the App's iCloud data through Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage. On Android, delete it through Google Play Games (Settings › Delete Play Games account data).
  • Achievements: Game Center and Google Play Games keep the achievements reported to them under your account there; you can remove that data through Apple or Google.
  • Analytics: Switching ANALYTICS off in Settings stops collection on that device straight away. Analytics events are anonymous and not tied to a name or an account, so there is no personal record for us to look up or delete. Firebase retains event data according to Google's retention settings.
  • Online rooms: Rooms close when the game ends, and the names and scores sent in them are not kept. The anonymous player ID is held by Unity; to have it deleted, email us at the address below and we will ask Unity to remove it.

Apart from anonymous analytics, we hold no data about you: no account, no profile, and no copy of your progress (the cloud backup is in your own account, not ours).

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy
