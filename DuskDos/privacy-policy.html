Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

Finish Line Labs, LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Dusk Dos mobile application (the "App"), available for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

In short: Dusk Dos is a free, single-player game with no in-app purchases and no advertising. We run no servers of our own. Your progress stays on your device and is backed up to your own iCloud or Google Play Games account, which we cannot read; achievements are also shown in Game Center or Google Play Games if you use them. We collect anonymous gameplay analytics (how runs and tables go) to balance the game, and you can switch that off in Settings.

Information the App Uses

Game Progress

The App stores your progress on your device: the run you are in the middle of (its map, deck, crew, Embers and shop), the islands you have cleared, your statistics (such as runs played and won and your best scores), the characters you have met in the Lore Book, your achievements, whether you have finished the tutorial, and your settings.

Cloud Backup

So that your progress survives a new or replaced device, and is the same on all your devices, the App keeps a copy of it in your own cloud account:

  • iOS: Apple iCloud, tied to your Apple Account.
  • Android: Google Play Games (Saved Games), tied to your Google account, when you are signed in to Play Games.

The copy contains the game progress described above. It does not contain your settings, contact details, or anything else.

These are your accounts, not ours. The data goes directly from your device to Apple or Google under their terms and privacy policies, and we have no ability to access, read, or retrieve it. You can turn off iCloud for the App in your device's settings, or delete your Play Games data through Google Play Games; the App then keeps your progress on your device only.

Achievements

The App has achievements (for example, clearing an island). They are kept on your device, and if you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the App also reports the achievements you earn to that service so they appear in your profile there. The App signs in automatically when you open it if you already use the service on your device; you can sign out or turn it off in your device's settings, and the App works the same without it.

The App receives only what it needs to show that you are signed in. It does not receive your email address, contacts, or friends list. Game Center is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google Play Games by Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, so that it can be balanced and problems found. This is limited to gameplay. The App can only send the following events, and there is no mechanism in it for sending anything else:

  • Starting, finishing or skipping the tutorial, and how far into it you got
  • A run starting and ending: which island, how many tables were cleared, and whether it was won
  • A table starting and ending: which challenger, the target, your score, and whether you won
  • The stops chosen on the island map, and what was bought or taken in the shop, at a market, shrine or campfire (which item, and its price in Embers)
  • A practice table starting and ending
  • Earning an achievement (which one), and opening How to Play, the Lore Book or Stats

Nothing you type is ever sent. Firebase itself collects standard technical information such as a randomly generated app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, and an approximate region derived from your IP address, as described in Google's Firebase Privacy documentation. The App does not collect or send an advertising identifier: the advertising permissions Firebase would normally add are removed from the app before it is built, and it does not use Apple's advertising identifier.

You can turn analytics off. Open Settings in the App and switch ANALYTICS to OFF. It is on by default; switching it off stops collection on that device immediately.

What the App Does Not Do

To be explicit, the App contains none of the following:

  • No in-app purchases, and no payments of any kind
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier
  • No crash reporting, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No third-party marketing, attribution, or social media SDKs
  • No account creation or login, and no collection of your name, email address or phone number
  • No online play or chat with other players
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts
  • No servers of our own, and no database holding anything about you

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information under any circumstances.

Artificial Intelligence

AI-powered tools (such as Claude by Anthropic) were used to assist with the development, artwork, and coding of the App. The challengers you play against are ordinary game logic running on your device. The App does not use or rely on any artificial intelligence or machine learning service, and your data is never sent to one.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: Uninstalling the App removes your progress from the device.
  • Cloud backup: Because the backup lives in your own account, you control it. On iOS, remove the App's iCloud data through Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage. On Android, delete it through Google Play Games (Settings › Delete Play Games account data).
  • Achievements: Game Center and Google Play Games keep the achievements reported to them under your account there; you can remove that data through Apple or Google.
  • Analytics: Switching ANALYTICS off in Settings stops collection on that device straight away. Analytics events are anonymous and not tied to a name or an account, so there is no personal record for us to look up or delete. Firebase retains event data according to Google's retention settings.

Apart from anonymous analytics, we hold no data about you: no account, no profile, and no copy of your progress (the cloud backup is in your own account, not ours).

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised effective date, and posted before the change takes effect in the App.
