Dusk Dos

Pusoy Dos, the roguelike. For iPhone and Android.

Climb the Isles of Dusk one card table at a time. Every table is a hand of Pusoy Dos against one of the islands' creatures, but here you don't win by getting rid of your cards: you win on points. Build big hands, swap for the cards you need, and reach the target before the challenger runs out.

In the game

  • Runs up the islands: choose your path on each island's map, past challengers, markets, shrines and campfires, to the boss at the top.
  • Your crew: hire the village's heroes with Embers, and level up your combos, to score bigger and bigger.
  • Creatures of Philippine folklore: the Kapre, the Tikbalang, the Bakunawa and more, each bending one rule of the table.
  • Lola's tutorial, a Lore Book that fills as you play, stats and achievements in Game Center and Google Play Games.
  • Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
