Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026 (updated for achievements and analytics)

Finish Line Labs, LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Pusoy Dos! mobile application (the "App"), available for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

In short: We run no servers of our own. Your progress stays on your device; achievements are also shown in Game Center or Google Play Games if you use them. Online play connects you to your friends through Unity's game services using an anonymous ID, and the name you type is shown only to the players in your room. We collect anonymous gameplay analytics (how matches and the Journey go) to balance the game, and you can switch that off in Settings. There is no advertising and no advertising identifier.

Information the App Uses

Game Progress

The App stores your progress on your device: the stars you have earned on the Journey, your lives and when the next one refills, the card back and table you have chosen, and your settings. It is not backed up to any cloud account and is not sent anywhere.

Online Play

If you choose to play online with friends, the App uses Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication, Lobby and Relay), operated by Unity Technologies, to connect the phones in a room:

  • An anonymous player ID. The first time you play online, Unity Authentication creates a random identifier for your copy of the App. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any account.
  • The room. Creating a room makes a short room code that your friends type to join. Unity's Relay service passes game messages between the phones in the room, and the room closes when the game ends.
  • The name you type. You enter a name to show at the table. It is sent only to the other players in your room, for as long as the game lasts. It is not stored by us or kept after the game.

Unity processes this connection information, along with standard technical information such as your IP address, under the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Playing alone (the Journey and Practice) does not use these services at all.

Achievements

The App has achievements (for example, clearing a chapter of the Journey). They are kept on your device, and if you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), the App also reports the achievements you earn to that service so they appear in your profile there. The App signs in automatically when you open it if you already use the service on your device; you can sign out or turn it off in your device's settings, and the App works the same without it.

The App receives only what it needs to show that you are signed in. It does not receive your email address, contacts, or friends list, and it does not send your game progress anywhere else. Game Center is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google Play Games by Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, so that it can be balanced and problems found. This is limited to gameplay. The App can only send the following events, and there is no mechanism in it for sending anything else:

  • A match starting and ending: whether it was Practice, the Journey or online, whether you won, how many deals it took, and your score
  • A deal ending: whether you went out first, and the points you took
  • Challenging a Journey stop, and the result: which stop, the stars earned, and the lives left
  • Running out of lives, and buying a lives refill
  • Making, joining or rejoining an online room, and taking over as host
  • Earning an achievement (which one), and opening How to Play

No names, room codes, or anything you type are ever sent. Firebase itself collects standard technical information such as a randomly generated app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, and an approximate region derived from your IP address; it also records in-app purchases made through the store, without any payment details. This is described in Google's Firebase Privacy documentation. The App does not collect or send an advertising identifier: the advertising permissions Firebase would normally add are removed from the app before it is built, and it does not use Apple's advertising identifier.

You can turn analytics off. Open Settings in the App and switch ANALYTICS to OFF. It is on by default; switching it off stops collection on that device immediately.

What the App Does Not Do

To be explicit, the App contains none of the following:

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier
  • No crash reporting, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No third-party marketing, attribution, or social media SDKs
  • No account creation or login, and no collection of your email address or phone number
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts
  • No chat between players
  • No servers of our own, and no database holding anything about you

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information under any circumstances.

Purchases

The App offers one optional in-app purchase: a lives refill, which tops your Journey lives back up to full. Checkout is handled entirely by the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store, through Unity's In-App Purchasing library. The store processes the payment and tells the App whether it succeeded.

We never see or store your payment details: not your card, billing address, or store account. Refunds and purchase history are handled by Apple and Google under their own terms.

Artificial Intelligence

AI-powered tools (such as Claude by Anthropic) were used to assist with the development, artwork, and coding of the App. The computer players you play against are ordinary game logic running on your device. The App does not use or rely on any artificial intelligence or machine learning service, and your data is never sent to one.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: Uninstalling the App removes your progress.
  • Achievements: Game Center and Google Play Games keep the achievements reported to them under your account there; you can remove that data through Apple or Google.
  • Analytics: Switching ANALYTICS off in Settings stops collection on that device straight away. Analytics events are anonymous and not tied to a name or an account, so there is no personal record for us to look up or delete. Firebase retains event data according to Google's retention settings.
  • Online play: Rooms close when the game ends, and the names typed in them are not kept. The anonymous player ID is held by Unity; to have it deleted, email us at the address below and we will ask Unity to remove it.

Apart from anonymous analytics, we hold no data about you: no account, no profile, and no copy of your progress.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
