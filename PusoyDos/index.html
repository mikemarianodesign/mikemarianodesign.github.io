Pusoy Dos!

The Filipino card game, for iPhone and Android.

Pusoy Dos is one of the Philippines' favorite card games, played around the table at family gatherings, fiestas and long nights with the barkada. Be the first to play all 13 of your cards: singles, pairs, triples and five-card hands climb from the 3 up to the mighty 2.

In the game

  • The Journey: travel the islands across four chapters of Philippine history, from Cebu and Mactan to the Revolution at Malolos, and beat a local card shark at each of 17 stops.
  • The faces of history: the court cards are 12 real Filipinos, from Lapu-Lapu to Jose Rizal and Andres Bonifacio. Win their portraits and read their stories.
  • Play with friends: make a room, share its code, and play with up to three friends.
  • Practice: play against three computer players any time.
  • Achievements in Game Center and Google Play Games. No ads.
